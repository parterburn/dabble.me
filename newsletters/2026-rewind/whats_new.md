Hi {{first_name}},

It's been a quiet year in your journal. That happens, and I'm not writing to nag you about it.

I'm writing because Dabble Me changed more in 2026 than in the previous several years combined, and you're paying for all of it. You should know what's there.

## A new look

Dabble Me has a new wordmark, a new set of icons, and a calmer palette. The account pages were rebuilt to match, so Settings and Security are easier to read and easier to use on a phone.

Nothing moved that you rely on. The daily email still asks how your day went, and replying still saves your entry.

## An iPhone app

There is now a Dabble Me app for iPhone and iPad. [Get it on the App Store](TODO-app-store-link)

It opens on Today, with a prompt to write and whatever you wrote on this date in past years. You can add photos straight from the camera, search your whole journal, and scrub through your timeline by month. There's a Home Screen widget and Face ID lock.

If email never quite fit how you write, the app might.

## Encryption for your journal

Your entries are now encrypted in our database, with the keys kept separately from it. A copy of the database on its own can't be read.

I want to be exact about what this is. It is not end-to-end encryption: our servers still decrypt your entries to email them to you, search them, and show them in the app. What it removes is the scenario I worried about most, where a leaked database would be a readable copy of everyone's journal.

The same protection now covers your posting address, your name, and your two-factor secrets. The code that does it is public, as all of Dabble Me's code is.

## Connect your journal to Claude or ChatGPT

PRO accounts can connect their journal to Claude or ChatGPT. Once you approve it, you can ask things like "what was I worried about last spring?" or save a new entry by asking.

It only ever works on your own journal, only after you approve it, and you can disconnect it at any time under Account Security.

## Smaller things that add up

- **Passkeys.** Sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device's passkey instead of a password.
- **A new posting address on demand.** If the address you email entries to ever ends up somewhere it shouldn't, you can reset it in Settings.
- **Day One import.** Bring your Day One journal over, photos included.
- **A yearly count instead of streaks.** Streaks punish you for having a life. Dabble Me now counts the days you wrote and leaves it at that.

## If you want to pick it back up

One sentence is a real entry. [Write today's]({{write_url}}), or reply to the next email with whatever is on your mind.

If the emails come at the wrong time, or too often, [change when they arrive]({{settings_url}}). A journal you write in twice a month beats one you feel guilty about every day.

And if Dabble Me isn't for you anymore, that's fine too. Reply and tell me why. It comes straight to me, and I'd like to know.
