Simpler Writing ideas: your template moves to the empty page, moments get a real row.

- Set a template in Settings → Entry Template (and leave "start new entries with it" off). Open a new entry: under "How has today been?" you should see "Use template" with a small "Swipe →" badge.
- Swipe right across the empty page: the template goes in, cursor at its first blank line. Try tapping "Use template" instead on another empty entry.
- Type a letter: the hint goes away. Delete it: the hint comes back.
- Check that tapping to place the cursor, selecting text and scrolling a long entry all work as before.
- Tap the lightbulb: no template button now. The prompt shows straight away, then today's calendar events, then "Add a moment from your iPhone" as a row with a photo icon.
- Touch and hold the lightbulb: iPhone moments open, with or without a template.
- Pick a moment from another day: you're still asked which day it goes on.
- Check it all in dark mode too.
