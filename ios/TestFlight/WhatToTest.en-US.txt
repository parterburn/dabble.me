Password fields now have an eye button to show what you're typing, and Sign Out and Delete Account have moved into Settings, Account.

Show password
- Sign out, tap Sign In, then Use Password Instead. The password field has an eye on the right, and the password is hidden (dots) to start.
- Type a few characters and tap the eye. The password shows as plain text, the keyboard stays up, and you can keep typing. Tap again (eye with a slash) to hide it.
- Tap in the password field: Passwords autofill should still offer your saved Dabble Me password, shown or hidden.
- Show the password, then swipe up to the app switcher. The snapshot shows dots, and back in the app the password is hidden again.
- With VoiceOver on, the button reads "Show Password", then "Hide Password" after you tap it.
- The same eye is on the password field in Settings, Account, Delete Account and in the Add a Passkey sheet (accounts with a password).
- Tapping the row around the field, not the eye, only puts the cursor in the field.

Account page
- Open Settings. Sign Out and Delete Account are no longer at the bottom; the list ends with Help, Privacy and Terms, and the app version below them.
- Tap Account (your email). Below Export Entries are Sign Out and Delete Account.
- Tap Sign Out. The dialog still offers Sign Out (this device only) and Sign Out of All Devices. Sign Out takes you back to the welcome screen.
- Sign back in, open Settings, Account, Delete Account. The page still shows your entry count, offers an export first, and asks for your password (or a code or passkey) before one last confirmation. Cancel it without deleting.
