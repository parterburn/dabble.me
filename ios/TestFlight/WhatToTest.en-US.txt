The editor's format bar: a way back out, the link buttons rearranged, B, I, U in the template editor, and a simpler Cancel.

GETTING BACK TO THE USUAL BAR

- Hold B with nothing selected: the bar switches to Link and B, I, U, with an X on its own at the far right.
- Tap the X: the bar should go back to photos, B, hashtags and prompts, and the cursor should stay where it was.
- Select some words: the X is there too. Tapping it should let go of the selection, leaving the cursor at the end of those words, and bring back the usual bar.
- Open the Link row and tap the X: the row should close and the keyboard go back to the entry.
- Tapping somewhere else in the entry should still go back to the usual bar, as before.

LINKS

- Select a link (or tap one). Beside Link at the bottom left there's now an external-link symbol instead of the word "Visit". It should open the address in the browser.
- The trash is now the plain system trash (red), at the far left of the row with the address, away from Update. It should remove the link and leave the words.
- Check that the trash, the address and Update all fit on one line on a smaller phone.
- A new link (Insert rather than Update) shows no trash.

TEMPLATE EDITOR

- Settings > Entry Template: the bar over the keyboard now always shows B, I and U, the same letters as the entry editor, instead of the Format menu.
- Each should be lit while it's on: for selected words, or with nothing selected, for what you type next. Tap one and type to check.
- Your saved template should be unchanged.

CANCEL

- Write something in a new entry, then tap Cancel: instead of the "Close this entry?" box, a small menu should drop down from Cancel with Save Draft and Discard (in red), and no other text.
- Save Draft should close the entry and bring your words back next time; Discard should close it and drop them.
- Tap outside the menu: it should close and leave you writing.
- With a photo added or removed, the first option reads "Save Draft (Words Only)": a draft doesn't keep photo changes.
- With nothing changed, Cancel should just close, as before.
