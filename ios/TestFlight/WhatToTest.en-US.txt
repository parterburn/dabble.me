Build 110 exports your entries from the app, by email, without signing in to the website first.

EXPORT FROM SETTINGS
- Settings, Export Entries (under Manage Hashtags). No web page should open. An alert should say we'll email your export to your account's address.
- The email should arrive like the one the website sends, with a link to download. The link opens dabble.me and asks you to sign in there before the download.
- Tap Export Entries again while it's still being made. The alert should say it's already being prepared, and no second email should come.
- Once the first email has arrived, tap it again. The alert should say we've emailed your latest export again, and the same link should arrive.

EXPORT BEFORE DELETING
- Settings, Delete Account. Email Me an Export should do the same, without leaving the page. Tap Cancel to keep the account.

OFFLINE
- With airplane mode on, Export Entries should say it couldn't start, and nothing should be emailed.
