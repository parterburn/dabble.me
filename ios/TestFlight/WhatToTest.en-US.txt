Build 121 asks before exporting your entries.

EXPORT
- Open Settings and tap Export Entries. An alert asks "Export Entries?" and says the email address the link to a ZIP of all your entries goes to.
- Tap Cancel. Nothing is sent: no spinner, no "Export Started" alert, and no email arrives.
- Tap Export Entries again, then Export. The spinner shows, then the usual Export Started (or Export Sent) alert, and the email arrives as before.

CHECK
- On the Delete Account page, Email Me an Export asks the same question first, and after Export its alert still says to download the export before deleting.
