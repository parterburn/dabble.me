Build 102 keeps the composer's Writing ideas row to two and a half calendar events, however long the prompt over them is.

WRITING IDEAS AND YOUR CALENDAR
- Pick a day with four or more events on your calendar (or set the entry's date to one).
- Start an entry and tap the lightbulb, with the keyboard up. Under the prompt you should see two events and the top half of a third.
- Tap to generate a prompt. The row should grow upward for it, and the events should stay where they are.
- Swipe up inside the row. It should scroll to the rest of the day's events.
- Tap an event further down. It should go into the entry as a heading, and the row should close.
- On a quiet day (one or two events), the row should be only as tall as what's in it.
