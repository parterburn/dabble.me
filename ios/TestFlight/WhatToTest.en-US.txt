Dictation crash fix: the app no longer closes the first time you tap the composer's microphone. iOS now asks for microphone access first, as it should.

First use (the crash)
- Best on a phone that has never let Dabble Me use the microphone (delete and reinstall the app, or use a new tester's phone).
- Open the composer and tap the microphone. iOS should ask to use the microphone, with "Dictate an entry instead of typing it." The app should stay open.
- Allow it, say a sentence, tap Done: the words land in your entry as before.

Saying no
- Reinstall, tap the microphone, choose Don't Allow. The app stays open and a note offers Settings to turn the microphone back on.

Still the same
- Camera, photos, calendar and Face ID ask for access as before.
