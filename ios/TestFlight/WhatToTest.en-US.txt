Free accounts now get an Actions button on an open entry, in the top-right spot where PRO accounts have the pencil, and On This Day shows under every entry with a tap through to upgrade.

Entry Actions on Free
- Sign in with a Free account and open any entry from Today or the Journal.
- Top right shows a bars icon (three stacked bars).
- Tap it. The entry's Actions sheet opens: Share, Delete (hold to confirm) and the entry's tags. There is no Edit and no Reflection row.
- Swipe left on the entry: the same sheet still opens, as before.
- Tap a tag in the sheet: it searches for that tag. Cancel a Delete without finishing the hold.
- With VoiceOver on, the button reads "Entry Actions".

On This Day on Free
- On a Free account, open an entry and scroll to its end. On This Day follows it on every entry: "N entries from other years would show here with Dabble Me PRO", or, with none, "See what you wrote on this date in past years with Dabble Me PRO".
- "Dabble Me PRO" is olive. Tap the line: your Plan opens in a sheet with the Monthly and Yearly options. Done closes it.
- Today's On This Day line on a Free account is olive and opens the Plan the same way.

PRO unchanged
- On a PRO account, the top right of an open entry is still the pencil. Tap edits; touch and hold opens Actions. On This Day under an entry shows only when there are entries from other years.
