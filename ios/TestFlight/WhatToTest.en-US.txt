Add a passkey: the app now offers to make a passkey for your account, so you can sign in with Face ID or Touch ID instead of your password.

- Sign out, then sign in with your email and password (on an account with no passkey). Once the journal loads, an "Add a passkey" sheet should come up. Tap Add a Passkey: iOS's own passkey sheet appears, and after Face ID the sheet says "Passkey added". No password needed, since you just typed it. You should also get a "passkey added" email.
- Sign out and use Sign In with a Passkey: the new passkey should work. It also shows on dabble.me/security, named "Added in the iOS app", and signs in on dabble.me.
- On an account with no passkey, tap Not Now: it shouldn't ask again on the next launch. It asks again after 30 days, or at your next password sign-in, and stops after three Not Nows.
- Settings → Account shows Add a Passkey while the account has none. Opened from there (or 15+ minutes after signing in), the sheet asks for your password first. A wrong password says "Incorrect password."
- Signing in with a passkey never shows the prompt.
