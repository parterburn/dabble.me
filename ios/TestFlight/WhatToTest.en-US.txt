Dictation: names spelled your way, without mixing up different people.

- If you tag a name with its own spelling (say #Katelyn), dictate "I had lunch with Katelyn." It should come out as Katelyn, not Caitlin or Kaitlyn. Try it with Polished and with Verbatim.
- A different name that only shares its consonants is left alone: with #Michael, saying "Michelle" stays Michelle; with #Danielle, "Daniel" stays Daniel.
- Ordinary words that sound close (Cattle, Kate, Catalina) stay as said, and so does a hashtag you say aloud.
- If you use two names that sound alike (#Caitlin and #Katelyn), dictation leaves whichever it hears alone.
