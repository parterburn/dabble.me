Passwordless sign-in: sign in or sign up with just your email and a code we email you.

- Sign out, then Sign In: the form asks only for your email. Tap the Email field: your dabble.me passkey shows above the keyboard. Pick it and you're in.
- Type your email and tap Continue: the next screen says "Check your email" and shows the address. Within a minute you get a 6-digit code. Tap the code above the keyboard (or paste it): it signs in as soon as six digits are in.
- On the code screen, "Wrong email? Change It" goes back with the address ready to fix. "Send a New Code" sends another; the first one still works for a few minutes.
- "Use Password Instead" shows the password field and signs in the old way. Passwords keep working.
- Type a wrong code: it says so and clears the field. Codes stop working after 10 minutes, and after a few wrong tries.
- Type you@gmial.com and tap Continue: it asks "Did you mean you@gmail.com?" first. Tap it to fix, or tap Continue again to send anyway.
- Start Your Journal with a new email: same two steps, no password, no name. The account is only made once the code is entered, so a typo leaves nothing behind.
- After signing in with a code, the Add a Passkey sheet comes up (as after a password sign-in). After signing up, it doesn't.
- With two-factor on, the code screen asks for your authenticator code after the emailed one.
- On an account made this way (no password): Delete Account and Add a Passkey (from Settings, later on) say "Email Me a Code" in place of the password.
