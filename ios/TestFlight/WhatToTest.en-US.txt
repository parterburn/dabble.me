Sign-in buttons: Continue never looks greyed out any more, and Sign In with a Passkey is now a plain olive link under it instead of a second button.

Sign in and sign up
- From the welcome screen, tap I Already Have an Account. Continue should be solid olive with the email field empty.
- Tap Continue with no email: an olive note under the field says "Enter your email first, and we'll send you a code." and the cursor goes to the field. Nothing is sent.
- Type "foo" and tap Continue: the note says it doesn't look like an email address yet. Typing in the field clears the note.
- Do the same from Start Your Journal.

Passkey link
- Under Continue, tap Sign In with a Passkey (olive text, no button outline). The system passkey sheet should open and sign you in as before.

Code screen
- Continue with a real email. On the code screen, tap Continue before entering the code: a note asks for the 6-digit code.
- Tap Use Password Instead, then Continue with the password empty: a note asks for your password.
- On an account with two-factor, after the password, tap Continue with the code empty: a note asks for your two-factor code.

VoiceOver (optional)
- With VoiceOver on, tapping Continue too soon reads the note aloud.

Still the same
- Entering a full email and tapping Continue sends a code, and a typo like gmial.com still gets "Did you mean…?".
- Passkey autofill above the keyboard still works.
