A day you didn't write is an open ring, not a faint filled circle.

- Today: the corner that opens Stats shows the last five days when you've written at least one of them. A written day is a filled circle. A day with no entry is an open ring in the same color. Size and spacing are unchanged.
- Stats: the same five marks sit beside the line that says how many of the last five days have entries. Written days are filled; days without an entry are open rings.
- Home Screen widget: under today's date, the same five marks. A written day is filled; a day with no entry is an open ring. Settings, Home Screen Widget shows the same preview.

If none of those five days has an entry, Today still shows your entry count instead of the marks.
