Entry text size: the three larger sizes are a little smaller.

- Settings, Theme, Entry Text Size. Large, Extra Large and Largest are now smaller steps up from Default (8%, 20% and 35% larger; they were 15%, 30% and 50%). Large should read as just slightly bigger than Default.
- If you had one of those sizes chosen, your entries will look a little smaller than before. Check the Journal, an open entry, and the editor.
- Small and Default are unchanged.

This build also updates the App Store screenshots kept with the project; nothing to test there.
