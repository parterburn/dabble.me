Dictation: hold the mic to talk, no title on the row, toasts clear of the buttons.

Hold to talk
- In the composer, press and hold the mic. Recording starts after a moment (the waveform shows). Talk, then lift your finger: it writes what you said into the entry.
- A quick tap still works as before: tap to start, then Done (or the mic again) to write it in, X to drop it.
- A slightly slow tap should still count as a tap, not a hold that stops the moment you let go.
- Tap to start, then press and hold the mic to stop: it stops, the same as a tap.
- The very first time (or with the microphone prompt up), letting go while it's still getting ready leaves it recording: stop it with Done.
- Hold and say nothing for 10 seconds: it stops with "Didn't hear anything".

Dictation row
- The row has no "Dictation" title anymore: just the dots while it gets ready or writes, and the X, waveform, timer and Done while recording.
- With VoiceOver on, the dots and the waveform read as "Dictation" plus the current step.

Toasts above the buttons
- Tap the mic and stay quiet for 10 seconds. "Didn't hear anything. Try again." should sit fully above the bar's buttons, with a small gap.
- The same goes for the microphone-off toast (with its Settings button) and the notice that hashtags were added.
