New: a "New Entry" control.

- Add it: open Control Center, touch and hold an empty spot, Add a Control, and search for Dabble Me. It can also go on the Lock Screen's bottom buttons, and on the Action button (Settings, Action Button, Controls).
- Tap it. Dabble Me should open with the composer up.
- Before 10 AM, with yesterday unwritten, the composer should be on yesterday; otherwise on today.
- Try it with the app closed completely, with it in the background, and with the Face ID lock on.
- "New Entry" should also be in the Shortcuts app under Dabble Me.

How long ago something was now reads the same everywhere: "2 years + 3 months ago".

- A photo's date band keeps its months: open a photo several years old and not near its anniversary. It should read like "14 years + 3 months ago", not "14 years ago", and the shared image should say the same.
- The date under an open entry's title, Today's Rewind, the widget and the push reminder all use "+" between the two units.

Writing an entry:

- There is a new Format button (an "A") left of the link button. It opens a menu with Bold, Italic and Underline. Select some words and try each; with nothing selected, it should apply to what you type next. Check the keyboard stays up and the selection isn't lost.
- Tap the "?" button. Your calendar's events for that day should be listed with no "ON YOUR CALENDAR" title above them. On a day with no events, nothing about the calendar should show at all.
- With calendar access turned off for Dabble Me, on an entry that has no prompt to show, the title should appear over the line asking you to allow access.

Settings, Calendars:

- The explanation at the top should sit close under the title, without a large gap above it.
- At the bottom there is a note on where to add or remove calendar accounts.

An open entry no longer sits in a rounded card.

- Open an entry that has other years under it (On This Day). The entry, and the bar above it, should be on the warm paper color, ending in a faint line; On This Day below should be on white (in light mode).
- Open an entry with nothing under it. The whole page should be the paper color, top to bottom.
- An entry's photo should run edge to edge.
- Pull the page down at the top: no white should show above the entry.
- Swipe left on the entry for its Actions, pinch to resize the text, and check both still work. Try dark mode and an iPad too.
