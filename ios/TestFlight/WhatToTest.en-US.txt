Beside an open entry, the list is now compact: on a wide screen (iPad, or iPhone Duo open), Journal and Search rows show a shorter preview with the photo as a small square at the side, since the open entry on the right already shows it full size. Regular iPhones are unchanged.

Journal on a wide screen
- Open the Journal on an iPad (or an open iPhone Duo). The timeline is on the left, an entry on the right.
- A row with a photo shows the photo as a small rounded square to the right of its text, not full width under it.
- Previews stop after about four lines with "Show more". Tapping a row opens it on the right, as before.
- A photo still processing shows a small placeholder in that square.
- Songs and dated #tags still show under the text.

Search on a wide screen
- Search for something with photo entries. Results use the same compact rows, and tapping one opens it on the right.

Narrow screens
- Make the app narrow (iPad Split View, or fold the Duo). The rows go back to the full preview with the photo large under it.
- On a regular iPhone, the Journal and Search look exactly as before.

Unchanged
- Today, On This Day and the Photos grid look the same.
- Everything from the last build (entries opening beside the list, folding and unfolding) works as before.
