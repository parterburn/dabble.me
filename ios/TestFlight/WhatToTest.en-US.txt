Sign-in buttons: the Sign In with a Passkey link now shows only on Welcome back (sign in), not on Start your journal, since a passkey can't create an account. Continue never looks greyed out.

Sign up
- From the welcome screen, tap Start Your Journal. There should be no passkey link under Continue.
- If you have a Dabble Me passkey on this device, tap the email field: your passkey should still be offered above the keyboard and sign you straight in.
- Tap Continue with no email: an olive note under the field says "Enter your email first, and we'll send you a code."

Sign in
- Tap I Already Have an Account. Sign In with a Passkey (olive text) shows under Continue and opens the system passkey sheet as before.
- Tap Continue with no email, then with "foo": an olive note asks for an email. Typing clears it.

Switching
- On Start your journal, tap I Already Have an Account: the passkey link appears. Tap New to Dabble Me? Start Your Journal: it goes away.

Still the same
- A full email and Continue sends a code; the code screen works as before.
