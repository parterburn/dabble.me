Build 125 lets you change your name in the app.

NAME
- Open Settings. The Name row (at the top, under Account) now opens a Name page in the app instead of dabble.me.
- With no name set, the row says "Add your name" and the page opens with the keyboard up on First Name.
- Type a first name and tap Done, or go Back. Settings shows the new name, and it's saved to your account: dabble.me's Settings page shows it too.
- Clear the first name and go Back: the name is removed and the row says "Add your name" again.
- An account that has a last name also gets a Last Name field (Return on First Name moves to it). Accounts without one don't see it.

CHECK
- Going Back without changing anything saves nothing.
- With no connection, tapping Done, or changing the name and going Back, shows "Couldn't save your name" and Settings keeps the old name.
- Tap Done, change the name again, and go Back right away: Settings ends on the newer name.
- The Email row still opens Account Security on dabble.me.
