Build 113 makes it clear that deleting your account deletes your export too, and stops a lost export from blocking a new one.

EXPORT BEFORE DELETING
- Settings, Delete Account. The text under Email Me an Export should say, in bold, to download the export before you delete your account.
- Tap Email Me an Export. The alert should say where it's going and that deleting your account deletes the export too.
- Tap Cancel to keep the account.

EXPORT FROM SETTINGS
- Settings, Export Entries. The alert should say where it's going, with no note about deleting.

THE EMAIL
- The export email should say that deleting your account deletes the export, so download it first.
- Use an account you don't need: export, then delete the account right away. When the email arrives, it should say the account is being deleted and the export goes with it.

STUCK EXPORTS
- Nothing to tap here. If an export never arrives, asking again after an hour should start a new one instead of saying it's still being prepared.
