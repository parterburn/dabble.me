Entries in the Journal list are now spaced like the full entry.

- Find an entry with several paragraphs, or headings with text under them (a template entry is ideal).
- Look at it in the Journal list, then open it. The gap between paragraphs should look the same in both: about half a line, not the bigger gap the list had before.
- The space between lines inside a paragraph should also match.
- Try it at the smallest and largest text sizes (Settings > Accessibility) and with the serif and sans fonts. The gaps should still match at each.
- A collapsed entry may show a few more words before "Show more" than it used to, because it takes a little less room. Expanding it and Show more should still work as before.
