Dictation is back to how it was: Type as I Speak is gone.

Dictation
- Open the composer, tap the microphone and talk. Nothing appears while you speak.
- Tap Done. The dots show briefly, then all your words go in at once, already tidied (with Polished on).
- Hold the microphone to talk and let go to finish: same thing.
- Settings, Dictation no longer has a Type as I Speak switch.

Logs
- Every dictation logs its timings under the "Dictation" category in the Console app.
