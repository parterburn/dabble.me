Build 119 makes dictation smarter, with no new settings.

YOUR SPELLINGS
- Add a name or term to Settings → Dictation → Custom Vocabulary (for example "Dabble Me"). Dictate a sentence with it said in lowercase. It goes in spelled your way.
- A hashtag you use that's a name (like #Leo) is spelled that way too when you say "Leo". A tag that's an everyday word (#Work) or joined-up (#LeoBirthday) doesn't capitalize your sentences.

SAY THE PUNCTUATION (Polished, English)
- Dictate: "Note to self colon buy milk exclamation point". It writes "Note to self: buy milk!"
- Try "open quote", "close quote", "dash", "open paren", "close paren", "question mark".
- Say "hashtag" before a tag you already use, even a joined-up one: "hashtag leo birthday" writes #LeoBirthday.
- Check prose stays prose: "It was a long period of time", "the comma is optional", "my period started" stay as said.

FIX MISHEARD WORDS (if you have it on)
- Dictate a question, like "should I call my sister tonight". It stays your question. It never answers it, adds "Here's the cleaned text", or cuts the entry short.

CHECK
- Verbatim still writes punctuation names as words.
- Other languages are unchanged.
