Build 99 fixes two things from the last few builds.

ACCOUNT SETTINGS
- Set an entry template and turn on "Start New Entries with It". Leave the Home Screen widget on its default.
- Pull to refresh, sign out, and sign back in. The template and the switch should both be back.
- Change only the Home Screen widget choice, then sign out and back in. The template should still be there, and the widget choice should be the one you just picked.
- Empty the template, sign out, and sign back in. It should stay empty.

DRAFTS AND START OVER
- Edit an entry, add a photo, and let it save a draft (swipe away, or tap Cancel then Save Draft).
- Open that entry again so it says "Restored your unsaved draft."
- Tap Start Over, then leave without writing anything else. Open the entry again: it should be the saved entry, with no restored-draft banner and without the photo you had added.
- A photo you add after Start Over should still be kept if you leave before saving.
