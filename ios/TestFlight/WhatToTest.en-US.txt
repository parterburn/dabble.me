Drafts now keep photos.

- Start a new entry, write a line, and add two photos (one from the library, one from the camera if you can).
- Tap Cancel: the menu should say just "Save Draft" and "Discard" (no more "Words Only").
- Tap Save Draft, then start a new entry again. The words and both photos should be back, in the same order, with "Restored your unsaved draft."
- Save it: the entry should have both photos.
- Edit an entry that has a photo. Remove the photo, add a different one, then Save Draft. Reopen the entry: the old photo should still be shown as removed and the new one there.
- Start Over on a restored draft should drop the draft's photos and bring back the entry's own.
- Add a photo, then swipe the app away without tapping Cancel. Reopen and start the entry again: the photo should be in the draft.
- A draft with only photos and no words can be saved as a draft too.
- Discard should still throw everything away, photos included.
