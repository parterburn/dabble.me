On a wide screen (iPhone Duo open, or an iPad), the Journal and Search now open entries beside the list instead of over it: the list on the left, the entry on the right. On any other iPhone, nothing changes.

Journal side by side (iPad, or iPhone Duo open)
- Open the Journal. The timeline fills the left half; the right half says "No entry open".
- Tap an entry. It opens on the right (a long one opens there rather than expanding in the list), and its row on the left is tinted olive. Tap another: it replaces the first.
- The entry on the right has no Back arrow. Swipe left on it for Actions, edit it, and scroll to On This Day as usual.
- Open an entry from its On This Day: it pushes inside the right half, with a Back arrow. Back returns to the first entry.
- Delete the entry on the right: the pane goes back to "No entry open".
- Write a new entry with +: once saved, it opens on the right.
- Photos and Songs open their entries on the right too. Stats uses the whole width.
- The timeline scrubber floats a little in from the middle line (rounded on both sides), not flush against it. Drag it and tap it as usual.

Search side by side
- Before searching, Search uses the whole width (tips and hashtags).
- Search for something. Results fill the left half; tap one to read it on the right.
- Tap a #hashtag in the open entry: the results change, the entry stays open.

Folding and unfolding (iPhone Duo, or iPad Split View to make the app narrow)
- With an entry open on the right, fold the phone (or narrow the window). The entry is now open full screen, with Back to the list.
- Unfold (or widen) again: the entry moves back to the right half.
- Open an entry while narrow, then go wide: it moves into the right half.
- The timeline keeps its scroll position through each change.

Unchanged
- On a regular iPhone, the Journal and Search work exactly as before.
- Today still opens entries full screen on every device.
