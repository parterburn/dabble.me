Build 115 adds Moments from your iPhone: Apple's Journaling Suggestions, inside the composer's lightbulb. iPhone only (not iPad), and only on a real device.

MOMENTS
- Start an entry and tap the lightbulb. Under Writing ideas you should see "Moments from your iPhone".
- Tap it. Apple's suggestions sheet opens: photos, places, workouts, music, people. The first time, iOS may ask to allow Journaling Suggestions (Settings → Privacy & Security → Journaling Suggestions).
- Pick a moment. Its title goes into the entry in bold, with a line for each thing in it, for example "📍 Golden Gate Park, San Francisco", "🏃 Outdoor Walk · 3.2 mi · 1 hr, 2 min", "🎶 Artist – Song".
- A moment that's a question from Apple (a Reflection) goes in as a bold question, like the lightbulb's own prompts.
- Before iOS 26, workouts say "Workout" instead of their name.

PHOTOS
- On a PRO account, a moment's photos join the entry's photos (up to 8 in all). Videos aren't added.
- On a Free account, the moment's words go in, and then a sheet says photos are a PRO feature, with the plans to subscribe.
- On a Free account, tapping the Photos button in the composer now shows that same sheet with the plans (it used to be an alert).

CHECK
- Nothing changes on iPad: no Moments row.
- The calendar events and the question still show in the lightbulb as before.
