Build 124 makes Restore Purchases clearer: it says what happened, and asks for your Apple Account password only when it has to.

Use a Free account. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox: nothing is charged.

RESTORE WITH A SUBSCRIPTION ON THIS DEVICE
- Buy PRO (Settings, Plan), then on another Free account, or after deleting and reinstalling the app, sign in to the account that bought it.
- Settings, Plan, Restore Purchases. It should turn PRO without asking for a password, and the Plan page should then show PRO.

RESTORE WITH NOTHING TO RESTORE
- On an Apple Account that has never bought Dabble Me PRO, tap Restore Purchases.
- Apple may ask for your Apple Account password. That prompt is Apple's own; the line under the button says so.
- After you sign in, it should say "No active Dabble Me PRO subscription was found for this Apple Account."
- Cancel the password prompt instead: nothing should be shown, and you can tap Restore Purchases again.

ANOTHER ACCOUNT
- With a subscription bought for a different Dabble Me account, Restore Purchases should say this Apple Account already pays for PRO on another account.

OFFLINE
- With Airplane Mode on, Restore Purchases should say the App Store couldn't be reached, or that Dabble Me couldn't be reached.
