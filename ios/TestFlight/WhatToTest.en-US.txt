Sign Out and Delete Account have moved from the bottom of Settings into Settings, Account.

Account page
- Open Settings. Sign Out and Delete Account are no longer at the bottom; the list ends with Help, Privacy and Terms, and the app version below them.
- Tap Account (your email). Below Export Entries are Sign Out and Delete Account.
- Tap Sign Out. The dialog still offers Sign Out (this device only) and Sign Out of All Devices. Sign Out takes you back to the welcome screen.
- Sign back in, open Settings, Account, Delete Account. The page still shows your entry count, offers an export first, and asks for your password (or a code or passkey) before one last confirmation. Cancel it without deleting.
