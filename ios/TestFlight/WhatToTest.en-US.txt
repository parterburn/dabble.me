Your entry template and Home Screen widget choice are now saved to your Dabble Me account, so signing out, reinstalling or a new phone no longer loses them.

FIRST LAUNCH
- Open the app once and pull to refresh. Your current template, "Start New Entries with It", and Settings > Home Screen Widget's choice should be unchanged, and are now copied to your account.

SIGNING OUT AND BACK IN
- Settings > Sign Out, then sign in again with the same account.
- After the journal loads, Settings > Entry Template should show your template again, with the same bold headings and blank lines, and the "Start New Entries with It" switch as you left it.
- Settings > Home Screen Widget should show the same choice, and the widget should follow it.

CHANGES
- Edit the template, leave the screen, sign out and back in: the edit should be there.
- Empty the template completely, sign out and back in: it should stay empty, not come back.
- Change the widget choice, sign out and back in: the new choice should hold.
- A widget set to its own choice (touch and hold it, Edit Widget) keeps that choice; only "Same as in the App" follows the account.

OTHER PHONES AND ACCOUNTS
- If you have a second device, a change made on one should show on the other after pulling to refresh.
- Signing in with a different account should not show the first account's template.

OFFLINE
- In Airplane Mode, edit the template, then turn it off and pull to refresh: the edit should still be there after a later sign-out and sign-in.
