Photo spacing
- Open an entry with a few paragraphs and a photo. The gap between the last line and the photo should look like the gap between two paragraphs, not tighter.
- Check the same entry on Today and in the Journal list: the photo under the text gets the same paragraph-sized gap.
- In Settings, Theme, under Entry Text, move the text size slider both ways and switch the font. The gap grows and shrinks with the text, still matching the paragraphs.
- An entry with dated #tags or songs above its photo keeps the smaller gap it had there.
- An entry with no photo looks the same as before.
