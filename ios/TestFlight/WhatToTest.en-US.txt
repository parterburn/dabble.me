The widget now takes turns through a day's On This Day entries.

- This is for the medium widget set to Rewind with entry previews on, on a day when you have more than one entry from past years.
- Through the day it should move from one to the next: they share 6 AM to midnight equally. With two, it changes at 3 PM; with three, at noon and 6 PM; with four, at 10:30, 3:00 and 7:30. Before 6 AM it shows the first.
- Each one has its own label ("3 years ago · 2023"), text and photo.
- Tap the entry on the widget: Today should open scrolled to the one that was showing.
- Open the app in the morning so the widget has the day's entries, then look at it again after one of those times. iOS may be a few minutes late changing it.
- A day with one past entry, or a widget set to a prompt or to Rewind without previews, is unchanged.

Nothing else in the app changed in this build. (It also updates the App Store listing text kept with the project.)
