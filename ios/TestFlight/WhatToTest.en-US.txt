Entry text size: each step is now 8%.

- Settings, Theme, Entry Text Size. The five sizes are 8% smaller than Default, Default, then 8%, 16% and 24% larger. (They were 12% smaller, then 15%, 30% and 50% larger.) Large should read as just slightly bigger than Default.
- If you had Large, Extra Large or Largest chosen, your entries will look smaller than before; on Small, very slightly larger. Check the Journal, an open entry, and the editor.
- These sizes add to the iPhone's own text size (Settings, Display & Brightness, Text Size), so with that turned up, entries are larger still.

Writing: the Format icon is filled in when the selected words are already bold, italic or underlined.

- Select a plain word: the "A" is an outline. Make it bold: the "A" fills in. Select words that are partly bold: filled in. Back on plain words: an outline again.

This build also updates the App Store screenshots kept with the project; nothing to test there.
