Three things: the editor's format buttons, the divider in entry previews, and the widget taking turns through a day's On This Day entries.

FORMAT BUTTONS

- Bold, Italic and Underline are now plain letters (B, I, U) and Link is the standard link symbol, not the drawn icons.
- Select some words: Link is now at the far left of the bar, and B, I, U are on the right.
- Select a link (or tap one): "Visit" and the trash are beside Link at the bottom left, away from Update. Visit should open the address in the browser; the trash removes the link. The row above has only the address and Update.
- Hold B with nothing selected: the bar should switch to Link and B, I, U. Tap I and type: it should come out in italics. The bar stays while you type, so you can turn it off again.
- Then tap somewhere else in the entry: the bar should go back to photos, B, hashtags and prompts.
- A quick tap on B should still just turn bold on or off, and lifting your finger after a hold should not toggle bold.

DIVIDER IN PREVIEWS

- Find an entry made of two merged together (the full entry shows a short line between them). In the Entries list, and in the preview when you hold an entry, the divider should be the same short line the full entry shows, not a bullet followed by two asterisks.

WIDGET

- This is for the medium widget set to Rewind with entry previews on, on a day when you have more than one entry from past years.
- Through the day it should move from one to the next: they share 6 AM to midnight equally. With two, it changes at 3 PM; with three, at noon and 6 PM; with four, at 10:30, 3:00 and 7:30. Before 6 AM it shows the first.
- Each one has its own label ("3 years ago · 2023"), text and photo.
- Tap the entry on the widget: Today should open scrolled to the one that was showing.
- Open the app in the morning so the widget has the day's entries, then look at it again after one of those times. iOS may be a few minutes late changing it.
