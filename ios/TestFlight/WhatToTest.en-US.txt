Entry text size: the default is now the middle of five.

- Settings, Theme: the Entry Text Size slider's stops are Smallest, Smaller, Default, Larger, Largest, with Default in the centre. Before, Default was second of five.
- The default is very slightly smaller than it was (1%), and each stop is a slightly bigger step than before (9% of the body size, was 8%).
- If you had chosen a size before this build, you should land on the nearest new one: Small becomes Smaller, Large becomes Larger, Extra Large and Largest both become Largest. Your entries shouldn't visibly jump in size, except from the old Largest, which comes down a little.
- If you never changed it, you're on Default.
- Pinch an open entry to step through the five sizes, and check the editor follows the same size.

An entry's preview and its Actions sheet now say how long ago it was.

- In the Journal, Today, Search or On This Day, touch and hold an entry. Above its text, next to the date, it should say how long ago that was as of today: "36 days ago", "3 years, 2 months ago", or "Today" / "Yesterday".
- On a long date the "ago" drops to its own line under the date. Check both look right, and that nothing is cut off.
- Swipe left on an entry for its Actions (or on the entry itself when it's open). The date at the top of that sheet should say how long ago too, beside it or on the line under it, and the buttons below shouldn't be cut off.
- Both should match what the entry says under its title when you open it in full.
