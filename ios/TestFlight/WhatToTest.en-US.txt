Build 116 sells Dabble Me PRO in the app (Monthly and Yearly). TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox: nothing is charged. In TestFlight a subscription renews once a day, whichever plan, and stops on its own after 6 renewals.

Use a Free account for these, not one that pays on dabble.me.

BUY
- Settings, Plan. You should see the Yearly and Monthly plans with their prices, Restore Purchases, and the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links.
- If a free trial is set up in App Store Connect, each plan should say "2 weeks free, then" its price (the first time this Apple Account sees it only).
- Tap a plan and confirm. The plan should change to PRO within a few seconds, and Search and Stats should open.
- You should get the "Thanks for subscribing" email.
- Settings, Plan should now say when it renews, with Manage Subscription (Apple's own sheet).
- Searching or opening Stats on a Free account shows the same plans under the PRO explanation.

RENEW, CANCEL, EXPIRE
- Come back the next day: it renews, and stays PRO.
- In Manage Subscription, cancel. Plan should say Ends instead of Renews, and PRO stays until then.
- After it ends (the next day), the account should go back to Free. Pull to refresh if it still says PRO.
- If a renewal fails (a sandbox card problem), Plan should ask you to update your payment details, with no Renews date, and PRO stays during the grace period.

UPGRADE
- On Monthly, open Manage Subscription and switch to Yearly. The plan should become Yearly right away.

RESTORE
- Delete the app, reinstall, sign in to the same account, then Settings, Plan, Restore Purchases. It should stay or become PRO.

ANOTHER ACCOUNT
- With an active subscription, sign out and sign in to a different Free account. Settings, Plan should say this Apple Account already pays for PRO on a different Dabble Me account, with no purchase buttons. Restore Purchases should say the same.

ACCOUNTS THAT PAY ON THE WEB
- Sign in to an account that's PRO through dabble.me. Settings, Plan should say it's through dabble.me, with nothing to buy. With a card subscription (Stripe), it should also say when it renews, or Ends if it's been cancelled on the web (allow up to 6 hours after a change).
- If that account's Apple Account is also paying for PRO, Plan should offer Manage App Store Subscription so the second subscription can be cancelled.

DELETE ACCOUNT
- With an active App Store subscription, Settings, Delete Account should tell you to cancel it in iOS Settings (deleting the account doesn't stop Apple's billing).
