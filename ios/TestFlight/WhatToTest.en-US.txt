Sign Out of All Devices
- Settings → Sign Out now asks: Sign Out (this device only, as before) or Sign Out of All Devices.
- Sign Out of All Devices signs out this app, every other copy of the app, and every browser signed in to dabble.me. Claude/ChatGPT connectors stay connected.
- Try it with the app on two devices, or the app plus a browser: the other one should land on the sign-in page within a request or two.
- With no connection, Sign Out of All Devices shows "Couldn't sign out of all devices" and leaves you signed in.
- On dabble.me, Account Security now has a Sessions section above Connected apps. The app shows up there as "Dabble Me app on iPhone" (or iPad) with a rough location and when it was last used.
- Sign the app out from that list on the web: the app should drop to the sign-in page on its next refresh.
- Plain Sign Out still leaves your other devices signed in.

Dictation: Custom Vocabulary shows your hashtags
- Settings → Dictation: under Custom Vocabulary, a new "From Your Hashtags" list shows the hashtags dictation listens for, as #Tag in gray. They can't be edited or deleted there.
- Your own words stay at the top, newest first, and still swipe to delete.
- Add a word that's also one of your hashtags (say "Leo" when you use #Leo): it moves up into Custom Vocabulary and leaves the hashtag list.
- With no connection, the hashtag list doesn't show. Everything else on the page works.
- Dictate an entry that names a Custom Vocabulary word and a hashtag: both come out spelled your way. Your own words are always sent first, so a long hashtag list never crowds them out.

Entry template on the empty page
- With a template (Settings → Entry Template), a new entry's empty page now shows it, faint, under "Start writing or [Swipe →] to use template:".
- Swipe right across the page, or tap the Swipe badge: the template goes in. Just start typing to write without it.
- A long template shows its first lines and fades out at the bottom.
- The "Start New Entries with It" switch is gone from Settings → Entry Template. If you had it on, new entries now open empty with the template showing, one swipe away, and Today shows the day's prompt again instead of your template (the widget too).
- Holding a Write button on Today still opens an entry with the template already in.
