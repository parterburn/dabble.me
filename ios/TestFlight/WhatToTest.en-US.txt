Dictation: quicker to start and finish, a haptic tap at start and stop, and centered loading dots.

Starting
- Open the composer: it should open and type exactly as fast as before (the model loads a moment after the keyboard is up). Wait a second, then tap the mic. The waveform should show almost at once, with only a blink of the dots first.
- Tap the mic straight away as the composer opens: it should still start promptly.
- After one dictation, start another in the same entry: it should be just as quick.

Finishing
- Say a sentence or two and tap Done. The words should go in noticeably sooner than before.
- Try a longer one (a minute or so): it takes a little longer, but should not sit on the dots for long.
- With Settings → Dictation → Fix Misheard Words on, it's still the slowest (the on-device model rewrites the text), but a bit quicker than before.

Haptics
- You feel a tap when recording actually starts (as the waveform appears, not when you touch the mic), and another when it stops: Done, the mic again, letting go of a held mic, the X, or the 10-second "Didn't hear anything".
- Hold the mic to talk: a tap once it starts recording, another when you let go.
- With System Haptics off in iOS Settings → Sounds & Haptics, there's no tap.

Centered loading dots
- While dictation gets ready, and after Done while it writes your words in, the dots sit centered in the row, where the waveform is while recording.

Still the same
- X drops the recording, and the next tap still starts quickly.
- Changing Settings → Dictation → Language, then dictating, uses the new language.
- The very first time on a phone (or after iOS clears the model), it still downloads the speech model first, with "Downloading N%" next to the dots.

Timings
- With the iPhone connected to a Mac, Console shows each dictation's timings: search for "Dictation".
