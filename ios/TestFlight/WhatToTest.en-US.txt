Dictation in the composer keeps one title.

Dictation title
- Tap the mic in the composer. The small olive title above the row says "Dictation" the whole time: while it gets ready, while you're recording, while it writes your words down, and on any error message.
- The row under the title still shows what's happening: dots and "One moment…" while it gets ready, the waveform and timer while recording, "Turning what you said into words…" after Done.
- With VoiceOver on, the title reads as "Dictation" plus the current step (Getting ready, Listening, Writing it down).
- The title matches the Dictation page in Settings.
