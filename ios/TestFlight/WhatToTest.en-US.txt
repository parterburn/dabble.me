Build 115 shows your template on Today and in the widget when every new entry starts with it.

TEMPLATE ON TODAY
- Settings, Entry Template. Write a template and turn on Start New Entries with It.
- Go to Today on a day you haven't written yet. The card should say Your Template and show the template's first two lines (blank lines skipped), ending in "..." if there's more.
- Tap the template. The composer should open with the template already in it.
- Write Today's Entry should do the same.

WIDGET
- With the medium widget set to A Prompt for You, it should say Your Template and show your template instead of the prompt, until today's written.
- Change the template or the setting in the app, then go to the Home Screen. The widget should follow.

PROMPT COMES BACK
- Turn off Start New Entries with It, or clear the template. Today and the widget should show the day's prompt again.
