Build 109 signs you in (password or passkey) and creates accounts with the app's own forms, and deletes an account right in the app.

SIGN IN
- Sign out (Settings, Sign Out). On the welcome screen, tap I Already Have an Account. A Sign In form should slide up, not a web page.
- Enter your email and password and tap Sign In. Your journal should open. Password AutoFill should offer your saved dabble.me login.
- Try a wrong password. You should see "Incorrect email or password." and stay on the form.
- With two-factor on: after the password, a Code field should appear. Enter the code from your authenticator app (or a recovery code) and tap Sign In.
- Forgot Password? should open dabble.me's reset page.

PASSKEYS
- On the Sign In form, tap Sign In with a Passkey. The system's passkey sheet should offer the passkey you made on dabble.me. Confirm with Face ID and your journal should open, with no password or 2FA code.
- Closing the passkey sheet should leave you on the form with nothing said.
- No passkey yet? The sheet should offer one from another device, or close it and use your password.

START YOUR JOURNAL
- Signed out, tap Start Your Journal. Enter a first name (optional), a new email and a password of 6 or more characters, and tap Create Account. The new, empty journal should open.
- An email that already has an account should say so, under the button.

DELETE ACCOUNT (use an account you don't need)
- Settings, Delete Account. A page should say how many entries go with it, offer Export My Entries, and ask for the password.
- A PRO account billed by Gumroad or PayPal should be told to cancel there.
- A wrong password should say "Incorrect password."
- The right password, then Delete Account in the last confirmation, should sign you out. The welcome screen should say when the account will be deleted (an hour away).
- Sign back in before then: the account should still be there, entries and all.
