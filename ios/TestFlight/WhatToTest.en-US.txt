Entry text size: the default is now the middle of five.

- Settings, Theme: the Entry Text Size slider's stops are Smallest, Smaller, Default, Larger, Largest, with Default in the centre. Before, Default was second of five.
- The sizes are closer together than before: 90%, 95%, 99%, 105% and 110% of the body size. The default is very slightly smaller than it was (1%).
- If you had chosen a size before this build, you should land on the nearest new one: Small becomes Smallest; Large, Extra Large and Largest all become Largest. From the old Extra Large or Largest your entries will get noticeably smaller, since the new scale stops at 110%.
- If you never changed it, you're on Default.
- Pinch an open entry to step through the five sizes, and check the editor follows the same size.

An entry's preview and its Actions sheet now say how long ago it was.

- In the Journal, Today, Search or On This Day, touch and hold an entry. At the top of the preview is the date in full ("Wednesday, November 12, 2025"), and on its own line under it, in a lighter color, how long ago that was as of today: "36 days ago", "3 years, 2 months ago", or "Today" / "Yesterday".
- Swipe left on an entry for its Actions (or on the entry itself when it's open). The top of that sheet should read the same way: the full date, then how long ago under it in a lighter color. The buttons below shouldn't be cut off.
- Both should match what the entry says under its title when you open it in full.
