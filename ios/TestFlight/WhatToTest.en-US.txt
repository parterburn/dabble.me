Each link on the Plan page now opens what it says.

Plan page links
- Sign in with a Free account and open Settings, then Plan.
- Tap Restore Purchases. It should restore (or say there's nothing to restore), not open the Terms or Privacy page.
- Tap Terms of Use: the Terms open. Tap Privacy Policy: the Privacy Policy opens.
- On a US App Store account, tap "Or subscribe on dabble.me": the subscribe page opens.
- Tap the empty space around the text in that box. Nothing should open.
- Tap Monthly and Yearly: each starts its own App Store purchase sheet.
- The same buttons on a PRO feature's page (Search or Stats on a Free account) still work as before.
