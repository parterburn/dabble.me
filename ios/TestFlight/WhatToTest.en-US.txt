App Store rating prompt: the app now asks for a rating at a good moment, right after you save a new entry, once you've written new entries in the app on 3 different days. It asks at most once per version, and never within 120 days of the last ask. iOS decides whether the prompt actually appears.

TestFlight never shows Apple's rating prompt, so here an "App Store Rating" alert stands in for it. In TestFlight it can come back once per build, so each build can be checked.

Check it
- Days count from this build on. Write a new entry in the app (Today or Journal, then Save) on 3 different days.
- On the third day, after Save: the composer closes, the entry opens, and about a second later the "App Store Rating" alert appears. Tap OK.
- Write another new entry the same day: no alert.
- Several entries on one day count as one day.

Never shown
- After editing an entry, or when a save fails.
- Over another sheet, or right after the passkey prompt.
- Entries written by email don't count.

Logs
- Every decision is logged under the "ReviewPrompt" category (Console app, filter by "ReviewPrompt"), for example "tooFewDays(2)" or "askedThisVersion".
