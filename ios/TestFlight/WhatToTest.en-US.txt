No changes inside the app in this build. On the website, signing in now offers a passkey once per browser, so the app's own passkey prompt is worth a quick check that nothing moved.

Passkey prompt (unchanged)
- On an account with no passkey, sign out and sign back in with your password or an emailed code: the "Add a passkey" sheet should still appear once signed in.
- Not Now puts it away; it shouldn't come back on the next launch.
- Settings → Add a Passkey is still there until the account has one.

Website (optional, on a Mac or iPhone browser)
