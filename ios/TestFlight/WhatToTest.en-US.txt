Entry template: hold a Write button to start from it.

- With a template saved (Settings, Entry Template), touch and hold "Write Today's Entry" on Today. The composer should open with your template in it, even with "Start New Entries with It" off. The same for "Write Yesterday's Entry" when it's showing.
- A plain tap still opens a blank entry (or the template, if that setting is on).
- With no template, a hold does what a tap does.

New: Share, in an entry's Actions.

- Open an entry and swipe left on it (or swipe left on it in the Journal and tap Actions). There is a "Share" row above Delete. It opens iOS's share sheet with the entry's date and words, and its photo if it has one.
- Choose Accessibility Reader there to have the entry read aloud. Does it read the words when a photo comes along with them? Copy, Print, Messages and the rest work too.
- An entry that is only a photo shares just the photo. One with only words shares just the words.

Holding an entry for its preview: there is no "Open" in the menu any more.

- Touch and hold an entry in the Journal: the preview comes up with Reflection, Edit and More under it, but no Open. Tap the preview itself to open the entry.
- The same for On This Day cards, a photo in Photos, and a written day in Stats or Jump to a Day: just the preview, which a tap opens.

Removed: reading a reflection aloud.

- The play button next to a reflection's X is gone. Everything else about a reflection (the follow-up chat, "Sum Up & Add to Entry") is as it was.

Also in this build, from the last one if you missed it:

- While writing, Format (the "A") and Link show only while words are selected. Format is a short menu: Bold, Italic, Underline.
- A web address pasted by itself goes in as a link.
- The hashtags row has an "Insert" button at its right for all the hashtags that fit. Holding the tag button tags only words that match hashtags you already use.
- The microphone button is gone.
