Free accounts now get an Actions button on an open entry, in the top-right spot where PRO accounts have the pencil.

Entry Actions on Free
- Sign in with a Free account and open any entry from Today or the Journal.
- Top right shows a bars icon (three stacked bars).
- Tap it. The entry's Actions sheet opens: Share, Delete (hold to confirm) and the entry's tags. There is no Edit and no Reflection row.
- Swipe left on the entry: the same sheet still opens, as before.
- Tap a tag in the sheet: it searches for that tag. Cancel a Delete without finishing the hold.
- With VoiceOver on, the button reads "Entry Actions".

PRO unchanged
- On a PRO account, the top right of an open entry is still the pencil. Tap edits; touch and hold opens Actions.
