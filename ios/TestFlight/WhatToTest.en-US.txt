Build 101 makes the composer's Writing ideas row a little shorter, so a busy day leaves more room for the entry.

WRITING IDEAS AND YOUR CALENDAR
- Pick a day with five or more events on your calendar (or set the entry's date to one).
- Start an entry and tap the lightbulb. The row should show the prompt, about three events, and the top half of the next one.
- Swipe up inside the row. It should scroll to the rest of the day's events.
- Tap an event further down. It should go into the entry as a heading, and the row should close.
- On a quiet day (one or two events), the row should be only as tall as what's in it, with nothing to scroll.
