Build 100 lets the composer's Writing ideas row scroll through the whole day's calendar.

WRITING IDEAS AND YOUR CALENDAR
- Pick a day with five or more events on your calendar (or set the entry's date to one).
- Start an entry and tap the lightbulb. The row should show the prompt and the first few events, with the scroll bar flashing once the events are in.
- Swipe up inside the row. It should scroll to the rest of the day's events, not stop at three.
- Tap an event further down. It should go into the entry as a heading, and the row should close.
- On a quiet day (one or two events), the row should be only as tall as what's in it, with nothing to scroll.
