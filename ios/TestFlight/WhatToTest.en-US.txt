Dictation: names spelled your way, even when they sound like a common spelling.

- If you tag a name with its own spelling (say #Katelyn), dictate it: "I had lunch with Katelyn." It should come out as Katelyn, not Caitlin or Kaitlyn. Try it with Polished and with Verbatim.
- Same for any one-word name in Custom Vocabulary or your hashtags (Stephen for "Steven", and so on).
- It changes only names: ordinary words that sound close (Cattle, Kate, Catalina) stay as said, and so does a hashtag you say aloud.
- If you use two names that sound alike (#Caitlin and #Katelyn), dictation leaves whichever it hears alone.
