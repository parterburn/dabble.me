Build 105 brings back speaking an entry, done the way Hex does it. Nothing is written while you talk. Tap Done and the whole recording is turned into words in one go, on your phone, then tidied.

SPEAKING AN ENTRY (iOS 26)
- Start an entry. The microphone is at the far right of the bar over the keyboard, after the lightbulb.
- Tap it. The first time, allow the microphone. It may download what it needs, once.
- While you talk, the row shows an X on the left, a waveform that moves with your voice, the time so far, and an olive Done on the right.
- Talk for a minute, then tap Done. "Writing it down" shows for a moment, then everything you said goes in at the cursor.
- Say the same thing with the keyboard's dictation. Is this clearly better: punctuation, names, fewer mistakes?
- Say "um" a few times, repeat a word ("I I went"), and correct yourself ("at 3, actually 4"). Those should be gone.
- Start again and tap the X. Nothing should be added.
- Type "We ate and", leave the cursor there, and speak. It should carry on the sentence in lowercase.
- Start talking and tap Save without Done. What you said should be saved too.
- Talk for five minutes or more. How long does "Writing it down" take, and is anything missing?

SETTINGS → DICTATION
- The switch turns the microphone off. It should disappear from the composer.
- Style: Polished (the tidying above) or Verbatim (as heard, ums included). Try each on the same sentence.
- Custom Vocabulary: add a name you say often that isn't a hashtag, then say it. It should be spelled your way. Your hashtags count already. Swipe a term to delete it.
- Language: Automatic follows your iPhone. Pick another language and speak in it. The first time, it downloads that language once. Polished only tidies English; other languages come out as heard.
- Fix Misheard Words (only with Apple Intelligence on, Polished, English): turn it on, then say a sentence the keyboard usually gets wrong. Is the result better, and is anything reworded that shouldn't be?
