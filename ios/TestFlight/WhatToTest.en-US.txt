Link: tapping back in your entry cancels it.

- While writing, select a word, tap the Link button and start typing an address. Then tap anywhere else in your entry. The address row should close, the word should not be linked, and the next time you open Link the address field should be empty.
- Inserting a link, and tapping an existing link to change or remove it, should work as before.

Share: Accessibility Reader should now read the entry.

- Open an entry, swipe left for its Actions, and tap Share. The entry now comes up on a plain page of its own (its date, its words, its photo) with the share sheet over it.
- Choose Accessibility Reader. It should show and read the entry's date and words. Before, it showed the date and the word "Delete": it reads what's on the screen under the share sheet, which was the Actions list.
- Try an entry with a photo, one without, and a photo-only entry.
- Other destinations (Messages, Notes, Copy, Print) should get the words and the photo as before.
- When the share sheet closes, the plain page should go away and leave you where you were. After sending to something, it stays a moment longer before closing.
- Sharing a photo from the photo viewer should work as before.

Also in this build, from the last one if you missed it:

- Touch and hold "Write Today's Entry" (or yesterday's) on Today to start from your template.
- Holding an entry shows its preview with no "Open" in the menu: tap the preview to open it.
- Reading a reflection aloud is removed.
