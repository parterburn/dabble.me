Moments from your iPhone (Apple's Journaling Suggestions) is easier to find, a reflection no longer adds its category to the entry, and Calendars moved above Theme in Settings.

Use an iPhone. Moments isn't on iPad.

FINDING IT
- Start a new entry and tap the lightbulb.
- Under the prompt and the day's events there should be a line, then MOMENTS FROM YOUR IPHONE in small olive capitals with an arrow, like the WRITING IDEAS heading.
- It should stay at the bottom of the panel while a busy calendar day scrolls above it.
- Edit an entry with no prompt, no template and nothing on the calendar: Moments should show without a line over it.
- Tap it: Apple's sheet opens.

ADDING A REFLECTION
- In Apple's sheet, pick a reflection (for example a Creativity Reflection).
- Its question should go in bold, the way a prompt does, with no "Creativity Reflection" line above it.

ADDING ANY OTHER MOMENT
- Pick a place, workout, photo or song moment. Its title should still go in bold, with a line for each thing under it, as before.

If Apple's sheet is empty, turn on Settings, Privacy & Security, Journaling Suggestions and its categories.

SETTINGS ORDER
- Open Settings. The App section should read: Entry Template, Dictation, Calendars, Theme, Shake for Random Entry, Require Face ID (or Touch ID or Passcode).
- Tap Calendars: it should still open the list of your phone's calendars, each with a switch.
